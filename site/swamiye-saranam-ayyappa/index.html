About the Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa

Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa is the call of surrender chanted by pilgrims to Sabarimala during the Mandala season and on the climb to the shrine. Devotees greet one another as “Swami” throughout the vow.

Best time to chant: During the Mandala vow (November–January), Makaravilakku, and daily prayer.

Benefits of the Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa

  • The refuge call of every Sabarimala pilgrim.
  • Repeated recitation can be used as a practice of attention and devotion.

How to chant the Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
