जय अम्बे गौरी, मैया जय श्यामा गौरी ।

Glory to Mother Ambe, fair Gauri; glory, Mother, to dark Shyama Gauri.

तुमको निशदिन ध्यावत, हरि ब्रह्मा शिवरी ॥

Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate on you night and day.

माँग सिन्दूर विराजत, टीको मृगमद को ।

Vermilion shines in the parting of your hair, with a tilak of musk;

उज्ज्वल से दोउ नैना, चन्द्रवदन नीको ॥

your two eyes are bright, and your moon-like face is lovely.

अर्थ

हे माँ अम्बे गौरी, हे श्यामा गौरी, आपकी जय हो। विष्णु, ब्रह्मा और शिव दिन-रात आपका ध्यान करते हैं। आपकी माँग में सिन्दूर और माथे पर कस्तूरी का टीका शोभा पाता है; आपके दोनों नेत्र उज्ज्वल हैं और चन्द्रमा जैसा मुख सुन्दर है।

Jaya ambe gaurī, maiyā jaya śyāmā gaurī

Glory to Mother Ambe, fair Gauri; glory, Mother, to dark Shyama Gauri.

Tumako niśadina dhyāvata, hari brahmā śivarī

Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate on you night and day.

Māṃga sindūra virājata, ṭīko mṛgamada ko

Vermilion shines in the parting of your hair, with a tilak of musk;

Ujjvala se dou nainā, candravadana nīko

your two eyes are bright, and your moon-like face is lovely.

Meaning

Glory to Mother Ambe, fair Gauri; glory, Mother, to dark Shyama Gauri. Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate on you night and day. Vermilion shines in the parting of your hair, with a tilak of musk; your two eyes are bright, and your moon-like face is lovely.

Jai Ambe Gauri, maiya jai Shyama Gauri

Glory to Mother Ambe, fair Gauri; glory, Mother, to dark Shyama Gauri.

Tumko nishdin dhyaavat, Hari Brahma Shivri

Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate on you night and day.

Maang sindoor viraajat, teeko mrigmad ko

Vermilion shines in the parting of your hair, with a tilak of musk;

Ujjval se dou naina, chandravadan neeko

your two eyes are bright, and your moon-like face is lovely.

Meaning (Hinglish)

He Maa Ambe Gauri, he Shyaamaa Gauri, aapki jai ho. Vishnu, Brahmaa aur Shiva din-raat aapka dhyaan karte hain. Aapki maang mein sindoor aur maathe par kastoori ka teekaa shobhaa paataa hai; aapke dono netra ujjval hain aur chandramaa jaisaa mukh sundar hai.

About the Jai Ambe Gauri

Jai Ambe Gauri is the best-known Hindi aarti of the Goddess, sung while waving the lamp in Navratri and at every Devi puja. Its opening praises the Mother on whom Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate day and night, then describes her adornment.

Best time to chant: Evening aarti, especially each night of Navratri.
