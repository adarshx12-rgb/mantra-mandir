Lord Ganesh

Shuklambaradharam

An opening prayer for a clear mind and the removal of obstacles.

Begin chantingMeaning & tradition

शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।

Clothed in white, all-pervading, moon-bright and four-armed;

प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥

meditate on that serene face, for the quieting of every obstacle.

अर्थ

श्वेत वस्त्र धारण करने वाले, सर्वव्यापक, चन्द्रमा जैसे उज्ज्वल, चार भुजाओं वाले और प्रसन्न मुख वाले देव का ध्यान करें, जिससे सभी विघ्न शान्त हों।

Śuklāmbaradharaṃ viṣṇuṃ śaśivarṇaṃ caturbhujam

Clothed in white, all-pervading, moon-bright and four-armed;

Prasannavadanaṃ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye

meditate on that serene face, for the quieting of every obstacle.

Meaning

Clothed in white, all-pervading, moon-bright and four-armed; meditate on that serene face, for the quieting of every obstacle.

Shuklaambaradharam vishnum shashivarnam chaturbhujam

Clothed in white, all-pervading, moon-bright and four-armed;

Prasannavadanam dhyaayet sarvavighnopashaantaye

meditate on that serene face, for the quieting of every obstacle.

Meaning (Hinglish)

Shvet vastra dhaaran karne vaale, sarvavyaapak, chaand jaise ujjval, chaar bhujaaon aur prasanna mukh vaale dev ka dhyaan karein, taaki sab vighna shaant hon.

About the Shuklambaradharam

The collection places Shuklambaradharam under Sri Ganesha stotra. The verse pictures a white-clad, moon-bright, four-armed presence with a peaceful face and asks that obstacles be quieted. The word viṣṇum here can be understood as “all-pervading”; the verse also appears in Vishnu-related prayer traditions.

Best time to chant: Any quiet moment you can set aside for attentive prayer. Browse the festival collection for associated occasions.

How to chant the Shuklambaradharam

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.

What does one count represent?

One count represents one complete recitation of the prayer displayed on this page. You can choose a short practice or count 108 repetitions.
