About the Om Gan Ganapataye Namah

Om Gam Ganapataye Namah, often written Om Gan Ganapataye Namah, is a short japa mantra to Ganesh. Gaṃ is the seed syllable of Ganapati found in the Ganapati Atharvashirsha. It is chanted before beginnings and as a daily practice.

Best time to chant: Morning, before a new task, and on Wednesdays and Chaturthi.

Frequently asked questions

Where does Om Gan Ganapataye Namah come from?

Ganapati mantra built on the seed syllable gaṃ from the Ganapati Atharvashirsha tradition. Some traditions recommend learning beej (seed) mantras from a teacher; many devotees also chant them simply with devotion.

What does one count represent?

One count represents one complete recitation of the prayer displayed on this page. You can choose a short practice or count 108 repetitions.
