विश्वंभरी अखिल विश्वतणी जनेता

O sustainer of the world, mother of the whole universe,

विद्या धरी वदनमां वसजो विधाता ।

bearing knowledge, dwell in my speech, O creator;

दुर्बुद्धिने दूर करी सद्बुद्धि आपो

remove my ill-thinking and grant me good sense;

मां पाहि ॐ भगवती भवदुःख कापो ॥

protect me, O Mother Bhagavati, and cut away the sorrows of this life.

अर्थ

हे विश्व का भरण करने वाली, समस्त विश्व की जननी, विद्या धारण करके मेरे मुख में वास करो। मेरी दुर्बुद्धि दूर करके सद्बुद्धि दो। हे माँ भगवती, मेरी रक्षा करो और संसार के दुःख काट दो।

Viśvaṃbharī akhila viśvataṇī janetā

O sustainer of the world, mother of the whole universe,

Vidyā dharī vadanamāṃ vasajo vidhātā

bearing knowledge, dwell in my speech, O creator;

Durbuddhine dūra karī sadbuddhi āpo

remove my ill-thinking and grant me good sense;

Māṃ pāhi oṃ bhagavatī bhavaduḥkha kāpo

protect me, O Mother Bhagavati, and cut away the sorrows of this life.

Meaning

O sustainer of the world, mother of the whole universe, bearing knowledge, dwell in my speech, O creator; remove my ill-thinking and grant me good sense; protect me, O Mother Bhagavati, and cut away the sorrows of this life.

Vishvambhari akhil vishvatani janeta

O sustainer of the world, mother of the whole universe,

Vidya dhari vadanmaan vasjo vidhaata

bearing knowledge, dwell in my speech, O creator;

Durbuddhine door kari sadbuddhi aapo

remove my ill-thinking and grant me good sense;

Maam paahi Om Bhagvati bhavdukh kaapo

protect me, O Mother Bhagavati, and cut away the sorrows of this life.

Meaning (Hinglish)

He vishva ka bharan karne vaali, samast vishva ki janani, vidyaa dhaaran karke mere mukh mein vaas karo. Meri durbuddhi door karke sadbuddhi do. He Maa Bhagvati, meri rakshaa karo aur sansaar ke dukh kaat do.

About the Vishwambhari Stuti

Vishwambhari Stuti, “Vishvambhari akhil vishva tani janeta”, is a Gujarati prayer to the Mother who sustains the universe. It is sung at aarti and garba during Navratri. The first verse asks her to remove ill-thinking, grant good sense and cut away the sorrows of worldly life.

Best time to chant: Navratri aarti and garba, and morning prayer.
