ॐ हौं जूं सः । ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः ।

Om and the sacred seed syllables; earth, atmosphere and heaven.

त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् ।

We worship the three-eyed Lord, fragrant and nourishing all.

उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात् ॥

Like a ripe cucumber freed from its vine, may I be released from death, but not from immortality.

स्वः भुवः भूः । ॐ सः जूं हौं ॐ ॥

Heaven, atmosphere and earth; the sacred syllables return in reverse order.

अर्थ

ॐ, हौं, जूं, सः। भूः, भुवः, स्वः। हम सुगन्धित और पोषण करने वाले त्रिनेत्र प्रभु की उपासना करते हैं। जैसे पका फल बन्धन से छूटता है, वैसे हमें मृत्यु से मुक्त करें, अमृत से नहीं। अन्त में लोकों और बीज अक्षरों का उलटे क्रम में आवाहन है।

Oṃ hauṃ jūṃ saḥ. Oṃ bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ

Om and the sacred seed syllables; earth, atmosphere and heaven.

Tryambakaṃ yajāmahe sugandhiṃ puṣṭivardhanam

We worship the three-eyed Lord, fragrant and nourishing all.

Urvārukamiva bandhanānmṛtyormukṣīya māmṛtāt

Like a ripe cucumber freed from its vine, may I be released from death, but not from immortality.

Svaḥ bhuvaḥ bhūḥ. Oṃ saḥ jūṃ hauṃ oṃ

Heaven, atmosphere and earth; the sacred syllables return in reverse order.

Meaning

Om and the sacred seed syllables; earth, atmosphere and heaven. We worship the three-eyed Lord, fragrant and nourishing all. Like a ripe cucumber freed from its vine, may I be released from death, but not from immortality. Heaven, atmosphere and earth; the sacred syllables return in reverse order.

Om haum joom sah. Om bhoor bhuvah svah

Om and the sacred seed syllables; earth, atmosphere and heaven.

Tryambakam yajaamahe sugandhim pushtivardhanam

We worship the three-eyed Lord, fragrant and nourishing all.

Urvaarukamiva bandhanaanmrityormuksheeya maamritaat

Like a ripe cucumber freed from its vine, may I be released from death, but not from immortality.

Svah bhuvah bhooh. Om sah joom haum om

Heaven, atmosphere and earth; the sacred syllables return in reverse order.

Meaning (Hinglish)

Om, haum, joom, sah. Bhooh, bhuvah, svah. Hum sugandhit aur poshan karne vaale trinetra Prabhu ki upaasanaa karte hain. Jaise pakaa phal bandhan se chhootataa hai, vaise hamein mrityu se mukt karein, amrit se nahin. Ant mein lokon aur beej aksharon ka ulte kram mein aavaahan hai.

Infallible Vedic Remedies (Mantras for Common Problems), Swami Shantananda Puri, enlarged edition 2008, contents entry 13, printed page 63. The enclosed verse is Rigveda 7.59.12; the surrounding seed syllables and reversed closing invocations belong to the extended recitation printed in entry 13. These additions are not part of the Rigvedic verse. Learn the seed-syllable pronunciation from a knowledgeable teacher. English meanings are paraphrases; punctuation and Devanagari spelling are normalized.

About the Mrityunjaya Samputita Mantra

This extended Mrityunjaya form places seed syllables and the three vyahritis around the familiar Tryambakam verse. The surrounding invocations distinguish it from the shorter Mahamrityunjay Mantra already in this collection.

Best time to chant: Any quiet moment you can set aside for attentive prayer. Browse the festival collection for associated occasions.

How to chant the Mrityunjaya Samputita Mantra

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
