जयन्ती मङ्गला काली भद्रकाली कपालिनी ।

Victorious, auspicious, Kali, Bhadrakali, bearer of the skull-bowl;

दुर्गा क्षमा शिवा धात्री स्वाहा स्वधा नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

Durga, the forgiving, the gracious, the sustainer, Svaha and Svadha: salutations to you.

अर्थ

जयन्ती, मंगला, काली, भद्रकाली, कपालिनी, दुर्गा, क्षमा, शिवा, धात्री, स्वाहा और स्वधा, इन नामों वाली देवी, आपको नमस्कार है।

Jayantī maṅgalā kālī bhadrakālī kapālinī

Victorious, auspicious, Kali, Bhadrakali, bearer of the skull-bowl;

Durgā kṣamā śivā dhātrī svāhā svadhā namo’stu te

Durga, the forgiving, the gracious, the sustainer, Svaha and Svadha: salutations to you.

Meaning

Victorious, auspicious, Kali, Bhadrakali, bearer of the skull-bowl; Durga, the forgiving, the gracious, the sustainer, Svaha and Svadha: salutations to you.

About the Jayanti Mangala Kali

Jayanti Mangala Kali opens the Argala Stotram, recited before the Devi Mahatmyam. In one breath it names the Mother as victorious, auspicious, Kali, Bhadrakali, Durga, the forgiving and the sustainer.

Best time to chant: During Navratri, on Kali Puja, and before reading the Devi Mahatmyam.
