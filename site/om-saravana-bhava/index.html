About the Om Saravana Bhava

Om Saravana Bhava is the Shadakshara, the six-syllable mantra sa-ra-va-ṇa-bha-va, chanted throughout Tamil devotion to Murugan. It recalls his birth in the Saravana reed pond, where the six Krittikas nursed him.

Best time to chant: Tuesdays, Shashti days, Skanda Sashti and Thaipusam.
