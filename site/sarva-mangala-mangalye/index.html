सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।

O auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, gracious one who fulfils every aim,

शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

O refuge, three-eyed Gauri, Narayani, salutations to you.

अर्थ

हे सभी मंगलों में मंगल स्वरूपा, कल्याणी, सभी उद्देश्यों को सिद्ध करने वाली, शरण देने वाली, त्रिनेत्री गौरी नारायणी, आपको नमस्कार है।

Sarvamaṅgalamāṅgalye śive sarvārthasādhike

O auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, gracious one who fulfils every aim,

Śaraṇye tryambake gauri nārāyaṇi namo’stu te

O refuge, three-eyed Gauri, Narayani, salutations to you.

Meaning

O auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, gracious one who fulfils every aim, O refuge, three-eyed Gauri, Narayani, salutations to you.

Sarva mangala maangalye shive sarvaartha saadhike

O auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, gracious one who fulfils every aim,

Sharanye tryambake Gauri Naaraayani namostu te

O refuge, three-eyed Gauri, Narayani, salutations to you.

Meaning (Hinglish)

He sabhi mangalon mein mangal svaroopaa, kalyaani, sabhi uddeshyon ko siddh karne vaali, sharan dene vaali, trinetri Gauri Naaraayani, aapko namaskaar.

From the Narayani Stuti, Devi Mahatmyam (Durga Saptashati) chapter 11. English meanings are paraphrases; punctuation and Devanagari spelling are normalized.

About the Sarva Mangala Mangalye

Sarva Mangala Mangalye praises the Goddess as the auspiciousness in everything auspicious and the one who fulfils every aim. It is recited during Navratri and at the close of many pujas.

Best time to chant: During Navratri, on Fridays and Tuesdays, and at the end of puja.

Benefits of the Sarva Mangala Mangalye

  • A beloved verse to the Goddess as the auspiciousness of all things.
  • Repeated recitation can be used as a practice of attention and devotion.

How to chant the Sarva Mangala Mangalye

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
