लक्ष्मीं क्षीरसमुद्रराजतनयां श्रीरङ्गधामेश्वरीम् ।

Lakshmi, daughter of the king of the ocean of milk, sovereign of Srirangam;

दासीभूतसमस्तदेववनितां लोकैकदीपाङ्कुराम् ॥

served by the celestial women, the radiant lamp of the world;

श्रीमन्मन्दकटाक्षलब्धविभवब्रह्मेन्द्रगङ्गाधराम् ।

whose gentle, gracious glance bestows splendour on Brahma, Indra and Shiva;

त्वां त्रैलोक्यकुटुम्बिनीं सरसिजां वन्दे मुकुन्दप्रियाम् ॥

I bow to you, mother of the three worlds, lotus-born and beloved of Mukunda.

अर्थ

क्षीरसागर की पुत्री, श्रीरंग की अधिष्ठात्री, संसार को प्रकाशित करने वाली लक्ष्मी को प्रणाम है। उनकी कृपादृष्टि से देवताओं को वैभव मिलता है। वे तीनों लोकों की माता, कमल में वास करने वाली और मुकुन्द की प्रिया हैं।

Lakṣmīṃ kṣīrasamudrarājatanayāṃ śrīraṅgadhāmeśvarīm

Lakshmi, daughter of the king of the ocean of milk, sovereign of Srirangam;

Dāsībhūtasamastadevavanitāṃ lokaikadīpāṅkurām

served by the celestial women, the radiant lamp of the world;

Śrīmanmandakaṭākṣalabdhavibhavabrahmendragaṅgādharām

whose gentle, gracious glance bestows splendour on Brahma, Indra and Shiva;

Tvāṃ trailokyakuṭumbinīṃ sarasijāṃ vande mukundapriyām

I bow to you, mother of the three worlds, lotus-born and beloved of Mukunda.

Meaning

Lakshmi, daughter of the king of the ocean of milk, sovereign of Srirangam; served by the celestial women, the radiant lamp of the world; whose gentle, gracious glance bestows splendour on Brahma, Indra and Shiva; I bow to you, mother of the three worlds, lotus-born and beloved of Mukunda.

Lakshmeem ksheerasamudraraajatanayaam shreerangadhaameshvareem

Lakshmi, daughter of the king of the ocean of milk, sovereign of Srirangam;

Daaseebhootasamastadevavanitaam lokaikadeepaankuraam

served by the celestial women, the radiant lamp of the world;

Shreemanmandakataakshalabdhavibhavabrahmendragangaadharaam

whose gentle, gracious glance bestows splendour on Brahma, Indra and Shiva;

Tvaam trailokyakutumbineem sarasijaam vande mukundapriyaam

I bow to you, mother of the three worlds, lotus-born and beloved of Mukunda.

Meaning (Hinglish)

Ksheerasaagar ki putri, Shreerang ki adhishthaatri aur jagat ko prakaash dene vaali Lakshmi ko pranaam. Unki kripa se devtaa vaibhav paate hain. Ve teenon lokon ki maataa, kamal mein vaas karne vaali aur Mukund ki priyaa hain.

About the Lakshmi Kshira Samudra

This four-line Mahalakshmi prayer praises Lakshmi as the daughter of the ocean of milk and the divine presence of Srirangam. Its imagery connects her grace with the splendour of the gods and the care of the three worlds.

Best time to chant: Any quiet moment you can set aside for attentive prayer. Browse the festival collection for associated occasions.

How to chant the Lakshmi Kshira Samudra

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
