About the Shri Devi Stuti

The Devi Stuti from the Devi Mahatmyam salutes the Goddess in one form after another, each time with the threefold refrain “namastasyai”. This page gathers nine of its best-known verses. For the single verse on śakti, power, see Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu.

Best time to chant: During Navratri, and on Tuesdays and Fridays.

Benefits of the Shri Devi Stuti

  • The Goddess in every being: as consciousness, intelligence, peace, faith and mother.
  • Repeated recitation can be used as a practice of attention and devotion.

How to chant the Shri Devi Stuti

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
