ॐ नमो भगवते धन्वन्तरये

Om. Reverence to the blessed Dhanvantari.

अमृतकलशहस्ताय

To him who holds the vessel of nectar in his hand;

सर्वामयविनाशाय त्रैलोक्यनाथाय

invoked as the dispeller of every illness and Lord of the three worlds;

श्री महाविष्णवे स्वाहा ॥

to the revered Mahavishnu, svaha.

अर्थ

ॐ। अमृत का कलश हाथ में धारण करने वाले, रोगों को दूर करने वाले, तीनों लोकों के स्वामी, श्री महाविष्णु स्वरूप भगवान धन्वन्तरि को नमस्कार। स्वाहा।

Oṃ namo bhagavate dhanvantaraye

Om. Reverence to the blessed Dhanvantari.

Amṛtakalaśahastāya

To him who holds the vessel of nectar in his hand;

Sarvāmayavināśāya trailokyanāthāya

invoked as the dispeller of every illness and Lord of the three worlds;

Śrī mahāviṣṇave svāhā

to the revered Mahavishnu, svaha.

Meaning

Om. Reverence to the blessed Dhanvantari. To him who holds the vessel of nectar in his hand; invoked as the dispeller of every illness and Lord of the three worlds; to the revered Mahavishnu, svaha.

Om namo bhagavate dhanvantaraye

Om. Reverence to the blessed Dhanvantari.

Amritakalashahastaaya

To him who holds the vessel of nectar in his hand;

Sarvaamayavinaashaaya trailokyanaathaaya

invoked as the dispeller of every illness and Lord of the three worlds;

Shree mahaavishnave svaahaa

to the revered Mahavishnu, svaha.

Meaning (Hinglish)

Om. Amrit ka kalash haath mein dhaaran karne vaale, rogon ko door karne vaale, teenon lokon ke svaami, Shri Mahaavishnu svaroop Bhagvaan Dhanvantari ko namaskaar. Svaahaa.

Infallible Vedic Remedies (Mantras for Common Problems), Swami Shantananda Puri, enlarged edition 2008, contents entry 2, printed page 35. The book prints this version without Vasudevaya and notes that some versions add it after Bhagavate. Devotional language about illness expresses a prayer, not a medical promise. About the Dhanvantari Mantra

Dhanvantari is revered as the divine physician and a form of Vishnu. This prayer describes him holding the pot of nectar and offers reverence to him as Lord of the three worlds. It may be recited for comfort during illness alongside appropriate healthcare.

Best time to chant: Any quiet moment you can set aside for attentive prayer. Browse the festival collection for associated occasions.
