About the Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah

Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah is a short japa mantra to Lakshmi. Śrīṃ is her seed syllable, connected with grace, beauty and abundance. It is chanted on Fridays, at Diwali and in daily home worship.

Best time to chant: Fridays, evenings after lighting a lamp, and during Diwali.

Lakshmi mantra built on the seed syllable śrīṃ. Some traditions recommend learning beej (seed) mantras from a teacher; many devotees also chant them simply with devotion.

What does one count represent?

One count represents one complete recitation of the prayer displayed on this page. You can choose a short practice or count 108 repetitions.
