About Us

Last updated: 19 October 2026

A place for daily practice

Mantra Mandir brings Hindu mantras, shlokas and stotras together with Sanskrit text, Roman transliteration, Hinglish reading aids and meanings. Our aim is to help you read attentively and build a manageable devotional routine.

Tools that stay simple

Browse by deity, festival or intention and use the manual 108-bead counter to follow your recitations. No account or subscription is required. Your saved practice stays in your browser.

Sources and editorial approach

The collection draws on published devotional sources and the books and teachings of pandits and spiritual teachers. Source notes identify references and variations where available. Meanings are editorial explanations; excerpts are labelled. This does not mean the website has been independently reviewed or endorsed by the authors of those sources.

Respect for different traditions

Wording, pronunciation and practice may vary between traditions. Follow your teacher’s guidance where appropriate. Devotional themes describe the purpose of a prayer, not guaranteed results.

Help improve the collection

We welcome corrections to Sanskrit, transliteration, meanings and source references. Visit Contact Us for our email address and the details to include with a correction.
