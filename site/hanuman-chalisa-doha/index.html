श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि ।

With the dust of my Guru’s lotus feet I cleanse the mirror of my mind,

बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु जो दायकु फल चारि ॥

and sing the pure glory of Rama, which grants the four aims of life.

बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार ।

Knowing myself lacking in wisdom, I remember the Son of the Wind;

बल बुद्धि बिद्या देहु मोहिं हरहु कलेस बिकार ॥

grant me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and remove my troubles and faults.

अर्थ

श्री गुरु के चरण-कमलों की धूल से अपने मन रूपी दर्पण को स्वच्छ करके, मैं श्री रघुवर के निर्मल यश का वर्णन करता हूँ, जो चारों फल देने वाला है। स्वयं को बुद्धिहीन जानकर मैं पवनपुत्र का स्मरण करता हूँ; मुझे बल, बुद्धि और विद्या दें और मेरे क्लेश और विकार दूर करें।

Śrīguru carana saroja raja nija manu mukuru sudhāri

With the dust of my Guru’s lotus feet I cleanse the mirror of my mind,

Baranauṃ raghubara bimala jasu jo dāyaku phala cāri

and sing the pure glory of Rama, which grants the four aims of life.

Buddhihīna tanu jānike sumirauṃ pavana-kumāra

Knowing myself lacking in wisdom, I remember the Son of the Wind;

Bala buddhi bidyā dehu mohiṃ harahu kalesa bikāra

grant me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and remove my troubles and faults.

Meaning

With the dust of my Guru’s lotus feet I cleanse the mirror of my mind, and sing the pure glory of Rama, which grants the four aims of life. Knowing myself lacking in wisdom, I remember the Son of the Wind; grant me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and remove my troubles and faults.

Shri Guru charan saroj raj nij manu mukuru sudhaari

With the dust of my Guru’s lotus feet I cleanse the mirror of my mind,

Baranaun Raghubar bimal jasu jo daayaku phal chaari

and sing the pure glory of Rama, which grants the four aims of life.

Buddhiheen tanu jaanike sumiraun Pavan-kumaar

Knowing myself lacking in wisdom, I remember the Son of the Wind;

Bal buddhi bidyaa dehu mohin harahu kalesh bikaar

grant me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and remove my troubles and faults.

Meaning (Hinglish)

Shri Guru ke charan-kamalon ki dhool se apne man roopi darpan ko saaf karke, main Shri Raghuvar ke nirmal yash ka varnan kartaa hoon, jo chaaron phal dene vaalaa hai. Khud ko buddhiheen jaankar main Pavanputra ka smaran kartaa hoon; mujhe bal, buddhi aur vidyaa dein aur mere klesh aur vikaar door karein.

About the Hanuman Chalisa Doha

The Hanuman Chalisa by Goswami Tulsidas opens with two dohas. The first cleanses the mind with the dust of the Guru’s feet before describing Rama’s glory; the second, addressed to Hanuman, asks for strength, wisdom and knowledge.

Best time to chant: Tuesdays and Saturdays, mornings or evenings, and on Hanuman Jayanti.

How to chant the Hanuman Chalisa Doha

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
