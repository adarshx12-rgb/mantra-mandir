बुद्धिर्बलं यशो धैर्यं निर्भयत्वमरोगता ।

Wisdom, strength, good repute, courage, fearlessness and health;

अजाड्यं वाक्पटुत्वं च हनुमत्स्मरणाद्भवेत् ॥

alertness and eloquence are traditionally said to arise from remembering Hanuman.

अर्थ

हनुमान के स्मरण से बुद्धि, बल, यश, धैर्य, निर्भयता, आरोग्य, सजगता और वाणी की कुशलता मिलने की पारम्परिक मान्यता इस श्लोक में व्यक्त होती है।

Buddhirbalaṃ yaśo dhairyaṃ nirbhayatvamarogatā

Wisdom, strength, good repute, courage, fearlessness and health;

Ajāḍyaṃ vākpaṭutvaṃ ca hanumatsmaraṇādbhavet

alertness and eloquence are traditionally said to arise from remembering Hanuman.

Meaning

Wisdom, strength, good repute, courage, fearlessness and health; alertness and eloquence are traditionally said to arise from remembering Hanuman.

Buddhirbalam yasho dhairyam nirbhayatvamarogataa

Wisdom, strength, good repute, courage, fearlessness and health;

Ajaadyam vaakpatutvam cha hanumatsmaranaadbhavet

alertness and eloquence are traditionally said to arise from remembering Hanuman.

Meaning (Hinglish)

Is shlok mein Hanuman ke smaran se buddhi, bal, yash, dhairya, nirbhaytaa, aarogya, sajagtaa aur vaani ki kushaltaa milne ki paaramparik maanyataa vyakt hoti hai.

About the Buddhir Balam

The concluding verse of Shri Hanumat Stotram names qualities traditionally associated with remembering Hanuman: wisdom, strength, courage, fearlessness, alertness and clear speech. Its reference to health is a devotional belief, not medical guidance.

Best time to chant: Any quiet moment you can set aside for attentive prayer. Browse the festival collection for associated occasions.

In your practice

How to chant the Buddhir Balam

  1. Read each line together with its meaning before beginning.
  2. Recite the complete prayer slowly, following the script you prefer.
  3. Use the counter after each complete recitation. Choose a comfortable number of repetitions.
